FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does a website cost?

See the packages above. Final price depends on pages and features, and I confirm it in writing before starting.

How long does it take?

Small sites often take a couple of weeks once content is ready. I give an estimate after discussing your project.

Will it work on mobile?

Yes. Every site is designed mobile-first and tested on common screen sizes.

How many revisions are included?

Each package lists its rounds of revisions. Extra changes can be quoted separately.

Do I need a domain and hosting?

Yes, they are usually separate yearly costs. I can suggest affordable or free options and help set them up.

Do you offer maintenance?

Yes, as an optional extra after launch, agreed separately.
